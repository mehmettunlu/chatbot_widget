# AcmeShop FAQ (English)

## Returns
Our return window is 30 days from the date of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Please include your order number with the return. Returns without an order number may be delayed or rejected.

## Shipping
We ship within 1-2 business days. Standard shipping costs $5.99. Orders over $60 ship free. Next-day delivery is available for $12.99.

## Payment
We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and bank transfer. Orders paid by bank transfer are held until payment is confirmed.

## Warranty
All products come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty. If your item is defective on arrival, contact us within 48 hours for a free replacement or full refund.

## Contact
Reach our support team at support@acmeshop.com or call 1-800-555-0199 (Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm EST).
